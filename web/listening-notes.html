When the right word leads to the wrong song

Imagine getting home after years away and finding yourself in tears at the door. Would you pick a breakup song for that moment? Before you make the playlist, ask what made the tears start.

1. What changed for the narrator?

In one historical entry we evaluated, a man stands before the palace after returning to office. His beard is wet with tears. Our search surfaced Baechigi’s “Shower of Tears,” a song about what remains after a breakup. Each narrator cries, but one has recovered something precious and the other is mourning its loss. In your own entry, try finishing the sentence “I felt this way because…” If that clause changes the song you would pick, the feeling word alone was too broad.

2. Who is the anger directed at?

In another entry a commander finds ships left unrepaired while the people responsible look after themselves. BIGBANG’s “Dirty Cash” moves the scene from ships to a society preoccupied with money. The setting changes, but the target of the anger survives: private gain at everyone else’s expense. Our evaluator gave it 2 out of 3. The song’s satire felt different from the commander’s stern resolve. A song can fit well without telling the same story.

3. Which feeling stays after the chorus?

For a diary about quiet contentment with family, “N분의1” earned only 1 out of 3. Sharing a meal made the comparison tempting. But celebrating a hard-won victory with teammates has a different energy from being quietly happy that nobody at the table is missing. When a single image makes a recommendation seem perfect, listen for what the rest of the track asks you to feel.

What happened when we tried two recommenders?

One evaluator considered three picks for each of the same 30 entries. They found about 38 of 90 picks acceptable from our Korean lyric search and reranking system, and 90 of 90 from our Billboard-catalog LLM service. These were different catalogs as well as different methods. This tells us what worked for this listener on those entries; it cannot predict what will fit your next entry.
