Operator and privacy contact

Feelody Support operates Feelody. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete your personal information, email shkid1004@gmail.com. Please do not include diary entries, passwords or sign-in links in your message.

Your journal and sign-in

Guest entries stay in this browser until deleted or browser data is cleared. Google sign-in connects your account ID, email, name and profile picture to a Feelody profile. Signed-in entries, moods and saved playlists are stored in Supabase (Australia). Cloudflare serves the site and processes requests. Authentication cookies keep you signed in.

Song recommendations

Saving a journal entry on the main calendar does not require sending it to the recommendation provider. When you enable recommendations and request songs, Feelody sends the entry and mood to its external model provider through Cloudflare. Asking for different songs also sends the previously recommended titles and artists. Avoid including information you do not want processed externally.

Optional activity history

When available, turn this on or off under Your privacy choices on this page. Account ID, time, sign-in/out and screen names help troubleshoot navigation. Declining does not restrict your journal. Records expire after three calendar months and are cleared from the live database on opt-out or closure.

Analytics and ads

The calendar and research pages use Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity only if you enable optional analytics in this browser. Only the public study and listening notes load Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to deliver and measure advertising. See how Google uses information on partner sites. Journal entries and recommendations are not included on these pages. The analytics switch below does not control advertising consent. Browser preferences are stored on this device. Turning analytics off stops future loading after reload; it does not erase data already sent.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete entries from the calendar. In the profile menu, Close account blocks access immediately; after 24 hours a scheduled task removes the account and saved journal data from the live database. Guest entries and provider backups/logs have separate lifecycles. Google sign-in does not delete or change your Google account.
