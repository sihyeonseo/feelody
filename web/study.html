Feelody / Study notes

Does reranking
help?

Can a song about friendship fit an entry about missing a dog? In our original K-pop experiment, reviewers judged the feeling, even when the situations differed.

Same candidates, different top three

We retrieved 30 tracks by diary–lyric similarity. One method kept the search order; the other scored each entry and lyric together. Reviewers rated emotional fit from 0 to 3. A score of 2 or 3 counted as a match.

A track the first pass missed

For an afternoon spent thinking about a dog, all three initial picks scored 0. BTS’s Friends rose from 15th to first and scored 2. Outsider’s track at search rank 23 rose to third and scored 3.

A track the second pass lost

For an entry about a day going wrong, the first retrieval result scored 2. It disappeared from the reranked top three, where all three tracks scored 0.

What the pilot tells us

Across ten entries, 4 of the 30 retrieval slots scored at least 2, compared with 10 after reranking. This small paired comparison does not measure user satisfaction or the accuracy of today’s Billboard recommendations.
