THE DIARY SESSIONS　/　VOL. 01

On the turntable

One entry, three songs

Write about a moment you want to remember. Feelody picks three songs from a selection of past Billboard Hot 100 charts. The story in a song can be different while the feeling is familiar.

  1. Pick a date and write. A moment, a conversation, or even one sentence is enough.
  2. Choose a mood. You can change the suggested mood before saving.
  3. Find your three songs. Read why each song was chosen, then open its YouTube search to listen.

Research note: What changes when the same songs are ranked again?

Where are my entries stored?

Signed-in entries sync to your account. Guest entries stay in this browser, even after a restart, but clearing browser data can erase them. Open a date and select Delete entry to remove it.

How are songs chosen?

When you request songs, your entry and mood go to our recommendation service. Your reflection and song notes follow the language of each entry. If you mix languages evenly, they follow your last substantive thought. The songs still come from the same chart archive. These are mood matches, not this week’s rankings.

Calendar colors reflect your own mood, not a diagnosis. Each music link opens a YouTube search for the artist and title.
